# Billing and refunds

Northwind Cloud bills every workspace monthly in advance, on the first day of the month.
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are always available under Settings, Billing.

## Duplicate charges

If you see the same charge twice, reply to your invoice email or contact support with the
invoice number. Duplicate charges are refunded to the original payment method within 5
business days of being reported. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund is
issued, and the duplicate line disappears from the next statement.

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of renewal. Monthly plans are not
refunded for partial months, but a downgrade takes effect at the next billing date. Refunds
always go back to the card or bank account that paid the invoice.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and the difference is charged pro rata. Downgrades take
effect at the end of the current billing period. Enterprise contracts are changed through
your account manager.

## Invoices and tax

Invoices show VAT or sales tax based on the billing address. Enterprise customers can request
consolidated invoicing across several workspaces and purchase order numbers on every invoice.
