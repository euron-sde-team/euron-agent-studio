# Support hours and service levels

Support is available around the clock for urgent incidents and from 8:00 to 20:00 CET on
working days for everything else.

## Response times

Enterprise customers receive a first response within 1 hour for urgent tickets and within 4
hours for high priority tickets. Business customers receive a first response within 4 hours
for urgent tickets. Starter customers are answered within one business day.

## Escalations

Urgent tickets from Enterprise customers are escalated to the on-call engineer and the
customer's account manager. A support lead reviews every reply sent to an Enterprise customer
about billing before it goes out.
