# Account access

## Resetting a password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and works
once. If the email does not arrive within five minutes, check your spam folder and confirm the
address with your workspace owner.

## Single sign-on

Workspaces on the Business and Enterprise plans can require single sign-on with Okta, Azure
AD or Google Workspace. When SSO is required, people sign in through their identity provider
and password sign-in is disabled.

## Locked accounts

After eight failed sign-in attempts an account is locked for 15 minutes. Workspace owners can
unlock it immediately from Settings, Members.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication can be enforced for the whole workspace. If someone loses their
device, a workspace owner can reset their second factor after verifying their identity.
