# Plans and pricing

Northwind Cloud has three plans. Every plan includes unlimited automations, the API and email
support.

## Starter

The Starter plan costs $15 per seat per month for teams of up to 25 seats. It includes the
shared inbox, automations and standard reporting.

## Business

The Business plan costs $39 per seat per month for teams of 25 to 250 seats. It includes SSO,
audit logs and priority support with a 4-hour response time.

## Enterprise

The Enterprise plan costs $69 per seat per month for more than 250 seats, with a dedicated
success manager, a 99.95% uptime SLA and data residency in the EU or the US.

## Discounts

Annual billing saves 15 percent on every plan. Non-profits and schools receive an additional
20 percent discount on request.
