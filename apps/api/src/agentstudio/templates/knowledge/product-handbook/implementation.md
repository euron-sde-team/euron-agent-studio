# Implementation and onboarding

Most teams go live in two to four weeks. Business customers get a guided onboarding with a
solutions engineer; Enterprise customers also get a migration plan and weekly check-ins until
launch.

## Integrations

Native integrations connect Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack and Microsoft Teams. Data from
Zendesk and Freshdesk can be migrated with full history, usually over a weekend.

## Training

Every new customer gets two live training sessions for agents and one for administrators,
plus access to the self-paced academy.
