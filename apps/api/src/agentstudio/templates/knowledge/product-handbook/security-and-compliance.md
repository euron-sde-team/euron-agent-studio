# Security and compliance

Northwind Cloud is SOC 2 Type II certified and audited every year. Customer data is
encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or later and at rest with AES-256.

## Data residency

Enterprise customers choose whether their data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US
(Virginia). Backups stay in the same region.

## Access control

SSO with SAML or OIDC is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. Audit logs record
every change to settings, automations and permissions and are kept for one year.
