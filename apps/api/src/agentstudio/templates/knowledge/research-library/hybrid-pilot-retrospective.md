# Hybrid pilot retrospective: Austin office

The Austin office piloted three remote days per week with 140 employees from March to August
2026.

## What worked

Productivity scores stayed flat while satisfaction rose by 11 points. Desk booking cut office
space needs by 30 percent, saving about $210,000 a year in rent.

## What to change

The pilot found that schedules drifted without regular check-ins. Managers should review
remote schedules in the HR portal every quarter. New joiners need at least three office days
per week during their first month to build relationships.
