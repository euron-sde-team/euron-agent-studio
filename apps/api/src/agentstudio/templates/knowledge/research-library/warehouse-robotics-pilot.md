# Warehouse robotics pilot results

Three warehouses ran autonomous picking robots for twelve weeks in 2026.

## Results

The Rotterdam warehouse reached 412 picks per hour, the best pick rate of the pilot, against
a baseline of 260. Error rates fell from 1.8 percent to 0.6 percent across all sites.

## Next steps

Operations should expand the pilot to the Lyon site in Q1 2027. Battery swaps caused most of
the downtime, so the next rollout needs a second charging station per zone.
