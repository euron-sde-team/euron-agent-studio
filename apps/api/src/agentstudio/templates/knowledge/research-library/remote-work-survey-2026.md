# Remote work survey 2026

People Operations surveyed 1,240 employees in September 2026; 81 percent responded.

## Results

Seventy-two percent of employees prefer a hybrid schedule with two or three office days per
week. Teams that agreed core collaboration hours reported 18 percent fewer meetings outside
working hours. The most common request was better video equipment in meeting rooms.

## Risks

Employees in customer-facing teams reported that ad hoc schedules made shift planning harder.
Managers should agree core collaboration hours with their teams before the rollout. Working
from another country remains the most misunderstood rule: one in four respondents did not know
it needs approval 30 days in advance.
