# Support operations review Q3 2026

The support team handled 48,200 tickets in Q3 2026, up 14 percent from Q2.

## Response times

The median first response time fell from 3.1 hours to 1.9 hours after AI-assisted triage went
live in July. Urgent tickets from Enterprise customers met the 1-hour target 96 percent of the
time.

## Recommendations

The team should add a weekend on-call rotation for billing questions, which made up 22 percent
of weekend tickets. Help center articles about invoices should be rewritten, since they were
the most viewed and least helpful pages.
