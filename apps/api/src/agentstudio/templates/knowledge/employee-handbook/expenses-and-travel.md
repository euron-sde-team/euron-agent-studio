# Expenses and travel

Book travel through the company travel portal. Economy class is the default; business class
is allowed for flights longer than eight hours.

## Meals and allowances

The daily meal allowance when travelling is $75 in the US and 60 euros in Europe. Alcohol is
not reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit receipts in the expense tool within 30 days. Expenses over $1,000 need approval from
your manager and the finance team.
