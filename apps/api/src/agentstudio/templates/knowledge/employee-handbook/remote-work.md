# Remote work policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their probation
period. Managers approve remote schedules in the HR portal and review them every quarter.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a monitor for home offices. Employees can claim up to $500
per year for home office equipment such as a chair or a desk.

## Working abroad

Working from another country requires approval from People Operations at least 30 days in
advance and is limited to 20 working days per year, for tax and legal reasons.
